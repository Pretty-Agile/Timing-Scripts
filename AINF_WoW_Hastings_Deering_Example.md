# AI-Enabled Ways of Working — Hastings Deering Example Proposal

## Phase 3: AI-Enabled Artefact Workshops

A series of facilitated workshops to produce the key artefacts that define and embed the new ways of working. Each workshop is structured around a playbook:

- Playbook 1: AI Opportunity Mapping
- Playbook 2: Prompt Engineering and Governance
- Playbook 3: Human-AI Workflow Design
- Playbook 4: Capability and Change Enablement

> **Caution — methodology exposure risk**
> This proposal named all four playbooks explicitly and described the facilitation model in enough detail that a capable internal team could replicate the structure without engaging Pretty Agile. In at least one case, a client appears to have done exactly that after receiving this proposal. Future proposals using this pattern should describe the outcomes and the structure of the engagement without naming every playbook or spelling out the facilitation logic in detail. The credibility comes from the diagnosis, the embed discipline, and Pretty Agile's experience running this — not from the playbook titles. Lead with what the team will have at the end, not how the sessions work.
